Common questions

Corporate training is built for a room of people with different roles and goals. Coaching is built around your specific situation — your team, your manager, your actual next step — which is why clients tend to say it's the first time the advice actually applied.

Most 1:1 engagements run six months, biweekly. Some clients continue longer once they hit a new role or challenge; others wrap up after the initial six months with what they need.

Most of my clients come from corporate and tech backgrounds, but the coaching applies to any mid-career professional navigating a leadership transition — the discovery call is where we figure out fit.

We spend 30 minutes talking through where you are, what's prompting you to look into coaching now, and whether it makes sense to work together. No pitch, no pressure — if it's not a fit, I'll say so.
